Extension Overview

What is Extoza Gmail App & Link Detector?

Extoza Gmail App & Link Detector is a specialized Chrome extension built to give users complete visibility over third-party applications, services, and associated external links linked or referenced through their Gmail ecosystem.

⌕

App Discovery

Scans and lists active application connections associated with your inbox sessions in an organized manner.

↗

Link Audit

Isolates external links and redirection endpoints found in connected notifications, allowing careful examination before clicking.

🛡

Security Control

Empowers you to identify unrecognized permissions or outdated application integrations and revoke access directly.

Privacy & Security Commitment

Strictly secure.
Complete data transparency.

We believe your inbox data belongs strictly to you. Extoza operates with a privacy-first architecture:

  • Local Processing: All connection scans and link analysis occur locally within your browser workspace. No sensitive email content or credentials are ever transmitted to external servers.
  • No Data Logging: We do not store, log, or monetize your inbox metadata, personal identifiers, or browsing activity.
  • Permission Transparency: The extension requests only the minimum necessary Chrome permissions required to inspect active tab links and side panel rendering.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What does the extension actually do?

It acts as a secure side panel workspace in Chrome to help you inspect third-party app connections and associated links found within your Gmail environment.

Is my email password or data shared?

No. Extoza processes everything locally on your device. We do not have access to your passwords, emails, or personal data.

How do I install the extension package?

Download the ZIP file from this site, extract it, enable Developer Mode in chrome://extensions, and click "Load unpacked" to select the folder.
